# WeTongbu 0.11.42

- Removed developer-only diagnostics from the settings page: device catch-up status, local sync performance statistics, the internal rebase inspector and the Vault ID copy button. They showed internal identifiers that are not needed for everyday use.
- Device management still lists your sync devices and lets you retire one, without the internal cursor details.
- The confirmation after approving a pending deletion no longer shows the internal change cursor.
